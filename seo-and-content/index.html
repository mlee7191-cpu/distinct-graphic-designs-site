SEO and AEO

AEO stands for answer engine optimisation. It means writing and structuring content clearly enough to be quoted by AI assistants and AI search results, as well as ranked in normal search results. Good SEO already covers most of it: clear pages, honest answers and clean technical foundations.

Questions

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can be picked up within days or weeks. Ranking for competitive searches usually takes months, so I report on the early signs along the way.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and no one honestly can. I focus on fixes and content that measurably improve visibility, and I report on them.
